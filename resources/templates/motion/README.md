# Original motion-graphic plates

Three original geometric RGBA assets authored procedurally for this repository,
rebuildable with `scripts/create_graphic_assets.py`. No third-party artwork,
fonts, logos, music or copyrighted scene templates are included. These assets
are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0 to the extent permitted by law.

The application inserts user-supplied title/subtitle/logo, renders actual alpha
MOV animation (fade or slide), and references the resulting local media in the
same editor. This is a small real asset pack, not a claim of 50 asset-rich packs.

Expanded to **12 original compositions**: the initial three plus split interview,
ribbon headline, bracket quote, double pill, corner frame, ticket announcement,
diagonal banner, stacked cards and orbit end card. These use different geometric
layouts, not merely palette variants. All support local title/subtitle/logo and
fade/slide rendering. They remain a focused geometric asset pack, not stock
footage, a 50-pack library, or a full layered motion-design interchange format.
